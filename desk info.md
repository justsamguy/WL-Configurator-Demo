As of September 20, 2026, WoodLab’s desk catalog supports the following answers.

### Pricing defaults

* **Base price: $4,250.** This covers American elm or similar wood at 60 × 36 inches, with a sit/stand frame and no tech package. Black walnut starts at **$4,750**.
* **Baseline size: 60 × 36, not 60 × 30.** Both configurable listings begin with Width 36 / Length 60.
* **Adjustable-height upcharge: not stated.** The separately listed UPLIFT V2 frame costs **$800**. Tops can be ordered without legs at an unspecified discount.
* **Provisional top-only estimate:** subtracting the $800 frame gives about **$3,450 for elm** or **$3,950 for walnut**. These are not reliable fixed-leg prices because they exclude alternative legs and installation.

### Adjustable-height details

* **Recommended label: “Sit/stand”**
* **Adjustable Height range: 26.5–52 inches.**
* **Preset heights:** none for adjustable desks. existing standard heights suffice for static height presets.
* **Configuration model:** adjustable height is a **base type replacing standard legs**, not a separate add-on. 

### Desk-size presets

Recommended short list:

* **60 × 36**
* **72 × 36**
* **84 × 36**
* **Custom size:**
* max width = 50"
* max length = 120"

### Compatibility limits

* the maximum length for a 2-leg desk frame is 84", any length above that will require a custom solution instead of a regular sit/stand base. in the 3D viewer, this base model cannot extend past 72" long.
* **Weight:** the separate two-leg frame is 75lbs on it's own.
* **Waterfall edges:** A floor-reaching waterfall leg would conflict with a rising desktop. It is only available with static bases.
